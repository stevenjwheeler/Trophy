# Trophy Trademark and Attribution Policy

## 1. Purpose

Trophy is an open-source software project licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 (GPLv3).

This policy exists to protect the identity, integrity and reputation of the Trophy project, prevent impersonation, preserve attribution to the original project and help users distinguish official Trophy releases from independently maintained versions.

It is not intended to restrict legitimate open-source development, redistribution or community contributions.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Trophy project's identifying marks, including:

- The Trophy name.
- The official Trophy logo and application icon.
- Official project branding and visual identifiers.
- Confusingly similar variants of these marks.

All trademark rights held by the Trophy project or its respective rights holders are reserved, except where permission is expressly granted by this policy or applicable law.

## 3. Permitted Use

You may, without requesting permission:

- Fork the Trophy source code for development, experimentation or contribution.
- Retain existing branding within development forks.
- Submit modifications and contributions to the project.
- Redistribute genuine, unmodified official releases, provided their origin is accurately represented.
- Refer to Trophy in articles, documentation, reviews and discussions.
- Identify another project as being based on, derived from or forked from Trophy.
- Link to official Trophy websites, repositories and releases.

These uses must not misleadingly imply official status, affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement.

## 4. Modified Distributions

Independently distributed modified versions of Trophy must be clearly distinguishable from official Trophy releases.

Unless prior written permission has been granted, such distributions must:

- Adopt a distinct product name and logo.
- Remove or replace official Trophy branding used to identify the modified product.
- Clearly identify themselves as unofficial and independently maintained.
- Preserve attribution in accordance with Section 5 of this policy.

The branding requirements do not apply to ordinary development forks, private modifications or contributions intended for inclusion in the official project.

## 5. Attribution Requirements

Pursuant to Section 7(b) of GPLv3, distributions containing Trophy's copyrighted material must preserve applicable copyright notices and reasonable author attributions identifying the original authors and Trophy as the originating project.

Attribution should include:

- Identification of Trophy as the original project.
- Acknowledgement of the original authors and contributors.
- A reference to the official Trophy website, where reasonably practicable.

An example of suitable attribution is:

> This software is derived from Trophy, originally developed by the Trophy contributors.
>
> Official project website: [Trophy website URL]

Attribution may be retained within the relevant source material or in the appropriate legal notices of the distributed software, such as an About or Credits page.

The wording and presentation may be adapted to accurately reflect the nature and extent of the original and subsequent contributions.

Attribution must not imply official status, affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement.

The preservation of attribution does not require the retention of the official Trophy logo, application icon or other visual branding.

These requirements apply only to the extent permitted by GPLv3 and applicable law.

## 6. Misrepresentation and Impersonation

You may not use the Trophy marks in a manner that misleadingly suggests official status, affiliation or endorsement.

This includes:

- Presenting modified software as an official Trophy release.
- Operating websites, repositories or download services that impersonate the official Trophy project.
- Using confusingly similar branding or domain names to mislead users.
- Misrepresenting the authenticity or origin of software distributed under the Trophy name.
- Using Trophy branding to disguise malicious or harmful software as an official release.

## 7. Relationship to GPLv3

This policy establishes additional terms concerning attribution, misrepresentation and trademark rights pursuant to Sections 7(b), 7(c) and 7(e) of GPLv3, to the extent permitted by GPLv3 and applicable law and authorised by the relevant copyright holders.

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict the freedoms granted under GPLv3, including the rights to use, study, modify and redistribute the software, or to restrict otherwise lawful use of the Trophy marks.

## 8. Permissions and Enquiries

Requests for branding permissions, questions regarding this policy, and reports of suspected impersonation should be directed to the Trophy maintainers through the official project's contact channels.
